Exercise science

Your muscles are talking to your brain, heart, and metabolism

Muscles do far more than create force. They release signaling molecules that influence inflammation, metabolism, and even brain function.

What myokines are

Myokines are signaling molecules released by working muscle. They help explain why exercise has effects that reach far beyond the gym floor.

Why it matters

Exercise can improve insulin sensitivity, support brain health, and reduce inflammation through these signals. In that sense, training is not just a mechanical act — it is also a biochemical one.

  • Muscle contraction signals other tissues.
  • These signals influence metabolic health and recovery.
  • Resistance and endurance work both contribute.

The practical takeaway

Training should be viewed as a full-body intervention, not just a way to change how you look. When you train consistently, your muscles are doing active work for your long-term health.

Your body does not separate “fitness” from “health.” The systems are deeply connected.
