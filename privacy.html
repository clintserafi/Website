Privacy Policy

How Performance Collective collects and uses information.

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, such as name, email, phone, referral source, and message details. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and provide the services you request.

Form submissions are delivered through our form provider and are not stored on this site. We do not share your personal information with third parties except when required to provide services or comply with the law.

If you contact us directly by email, we will use that email solely to respond to your inquiry and manage the relationship with you.

For security reasons and to reduce spam, the contact form includes anti-spam checks and required fields. If you need help, email hello@performancecollective.co.
