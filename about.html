Meet the Team

Two clinicians dedicated to clear health navigation and better performance outcomes.

Clint Serafino

Founder • Clinical Coach

Dr. Clint Serafino is a dedicated and experienced Physical Therapist with over 14 years of expertise. He has worked with the highest levels of the US Military, including Rangers, Green Berets, SEALs, and the Special Operations Command in Germany. In 2017, he completed a Fellowship in Orthopedic Manual Physical Therapy, which puts Clint in the top 1% of Physical Therapists. Throughout his career, he provided technical and clinical expertise to professional athletes in the MLS, NASCAR, and Professional Bull Riding, as well as NCAA Division I track and field and soccer athletes.

Clint is renowned for his ability to identify the root cause of pain and dysfunction and deliver the most effective interventions to address both. He serves as a Senior Faculty for the American Academy of Manipulative Therapy, and has taught internationally as a Senior Instructor with the Dry Needling and Spinal Manipulation Institutes. Since 2017, he has been a Fellow of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Manual Physical Therapy (FAAOMPT). Clint has mentored Fellows, entry-level PTs and OTs, and physical therapy students at every stage of their professional journey.

Clint has taken an active role in his own health and longevity, developing an annual training plan built on evidence-informed concepts, minimal effective dose training, and objective data tracking for strength, body composition, and movement competency. As a Physical Therapist, Clint empowers active members of our community to navigate the healthcare system and the performance space to experience real, long-term transformation.

Jennifer McConnell

Clinical Support Partner

Through dedicated service as a Physical Therapist Assistant, Jenn has become a trusted figure in the field of rehabilitation and sports performance. As a certified Performance Enhancement Specialist, she has honed her skills working with hundreds of athletes in the NFL and MLS, Division I collegiate athletics, and thousands of members of the community recovering from orthopedic surgery.

Jennifer has played an integral role in getting athletes back to the highest level of competition. In addition to opening her own clinic in 2024, she added a Pre-/Post-Partum strength and conditioning certification to her skillset — helping moms regain strength, mobility, endurance, and confidence before and after their baby arrives.

Jennifer is committed to her own health and longevity through resistance training and cardiorespiratory fitness. She's a running coach who competes in local 5K and 10K events. Whether it's on the field, in the gym, or in everyday life, Jennifer is dedicated to empowering our community to make the short-term changes that add up to long-term transformation.
