Nutrition

Meat, muscle, and body composition

Lean protein is one of the most practical tools available for preserving muscle, controlling appetite, and supporting performance.

Why protein quality matters

Not all protein is created equal. High-quality protein provides all the essential amino acids that your body needs to build and maintain muscle tissue, and it does so efficiently.

Lean meat is especially useful because it offers complete protein plus nutrients that are difficult to get in large amounts from plant foods alone.

How it helps body composition

During fat loss, adequate protein helps preserve lean mass. During muscle gain, it supports recovery and growth. It also helps with satiety, making it easier to eat in a controlled way without feeling deprived.

  • Supports muscle retention during a calorie deficit.
  • Improves recovery after resistance training.
  • Helps regulate hunger and improve adherence.

Practical guidance

Choose lean, minimally processed sources and pair them with vegetables, fruit, and quality carbohydrates. For many adults, aiming for a solid protein intake across the day is more important than overcomplicating the plan.

Simple nutrition often works best when the basics are consistent.
