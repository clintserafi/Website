Questions

Before you sign on

Is this a replacement for my prosthetist or physical therapist?

No. This is health navigation and coaching — it complements your care team and refers back into it whenever hands-on care, fitting adjustments, or diagnosis is needed.

I'm already “cleared.” Why would I need this?

Because cleared and capable of your specific goal aren't the same thing. Cleared means safe to move. This is about building toward what you actually want to do.

What if I only need occasional guidance?

That's exactly what Tier 1 is for — a standing relationship you can call on without committing to a full structured plan.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes. Many members start on Tier 1 and move to Tier 2 once a specific goal — a 5K, a season, a project — needs structured programming.

Does this include hormone, metabolic, or supplement support?

That level of physician-managed support lives in Tier 3, coordinated through our physician network.
