Questions

Before you sign on

I'm breastfeeding — does the nutrition guidance still work?

Yes. Plans are built to account for feeding status either way, adjusting as that status changes.

Is this instead of my OB or a pelvic floor specialist?

No. This is health navigation and coaching — it complements your care team and refers into it whenever hands-on evaluation is needed.

What if I only need occasional guidance?

That's Tier 1 — a standing relationship without committing to a full structured plan.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes, as your recovery and goals change.

Does this include hormone or metabolic support?

That level of physician-managed support lives in Tier 3, coordinated through our physician network.
