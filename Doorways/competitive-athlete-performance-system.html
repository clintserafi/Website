The Framework

Three categories. Most athletes are only covering one.

01

To your body.

Self soft-tissue mobilization, recovery tools like MyoPux, low-intensity active recovery — the maintenance most athletes skip until something breaks.

02

With your body.

Corrective movement, activation drills, kinetic chain work — the stuff that makes your sport-specific training actually stick.

03

For your body.

Sleep hygiene, meditation, visualization, and nutrition support — the invisible work that determines whether the first two hold up.

Questions

Before you sign on

Does this replace my team's athletic trainer or team physician?

No. This is health navigation and coaching, focused on the gaps between sessions — it complements your team's medical staff, not replaces them.

Is this sport-specific?

The framework — to/with/for your body — applies across sports. The programming inside it is built around your specific sport and season.

What if I only need occasional guidance?

That's Tier 1 — a standing relationship without committing to a full structured plan.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes, many athletes scale up around competition season and back down in the off-season.

Does this include supplement or recovery-tool guidance?

Structured guidance is part of Tier 2 and Tier 3, with physician-level supplementation support in Tier 3.
