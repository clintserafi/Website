Questions

Before you sign on

I've already tried PT and surgery — what's different here?

This isn't another treatment attempt. It's coordination across everything you've already tried, so the next step actually makes sense given the whole picture.

Can you get my providers to actually talk to each other?

I can't compel your specialists to collaborate directly, but I can help you understand how their input connects, ask sharper questions at your next appointment, and build a plan that accounts for the whole picture instead of one piece of it.

Is this specific to CRPS?

It's built for chronic, complex, or multi-specialty pain situations generally — CRPS is one example, not the only one.

What if I only need occasional guidance?

That's Tier 1 — a standing relationship without committing to a full structured plan.

Does this include hormone or metabolic support?

That level of physician-managed support lives in Tier 3, coordinated through our physician network.
