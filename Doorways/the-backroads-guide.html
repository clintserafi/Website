Questions

Before you sign on

Is this instead of a doctor or the ER?

No. Your Backroads Guide helps you figure out whether you need one — and stays in your corner if you do. In a true emergency, always go to the ER first.

Do I need insurance for this?

No. Membership works the same whether you're parked in Utah or Maine, insured or not.

What if I only need this occasionally?

That's exactly what Health Navigation Retainer is for.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes — many members start on Health Navigation Retainer and move up once a specific injury or goal needs a structured plan.

Does this cover nutrition, sleep, and mental health too?

Coaching for Performance includes sleep, nutrition, and lifestyle coaching, plus strength and conditioning. For the mental load of this lifestyle, your Backroads Guide screens for it and loops in the right licensed professional when needed — the same biopsychosocial approach used in modern physical therapy.

Does this include hormone, metabolic, or supplement support?

That level of physician-managed support lives in Tier 3, coordinated through our physician network.
